FamFlow Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 9, 2026

Overview

FamFlow ("the App") is a family planning and organization application designed to help parents manage daily routines, schedules, activities, and budgets. Your privacy is important to us, and this policy explains how the App handles your information.

Data Collection

FamFlow does NOT collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers.
All data you enter into the App is stored exclusively on your device using local storage.

What Data is Stored Locally

The following types of information are stored on your device only:

  • Family Profile Information: Names and ages of family members
  • Schedule Data: School schedules, daily routines, activity times
  • Activity Information: Extracurricular activities, appointments
  • Budget Data: Income, expense categories, financial goals
  • Preferences: App settings, pain points, notification preferences
  • Profile Configurations: Multiple family profiles and their associated data
  • Task Lists: To-do items and their completion status

Data Sharing

FamFlow does NOT share your data with any third parties. Specifically:

  • No data is sent to external servers
  • No analytics or tracking services are used
  • No advertising networks receive your information
  • No data is sold, rented, or traded to any entity
  • No cloud synchronization services are used

Data Storage & Security

  • All data is stored locally on your device using AsyncStorage
  • Data persists between app sessions on your device
  • Data is only accessible within the FamFlow app on your device
  • Uninstalling the app will permanently delete all stored data

Children's Privacy

FamFlow is designed for use by parents and guardians. While the app stores children's names and ages as part of family profiles, this data:

  • Is entered voluntarily by the parent/guardian
  • Never leaves the device
  • Is never transmitted to any server or third party
  • Is fully controlled by the parent/guardian who can edit or delete it at any time

Permissions

FamFlow may request the following device permissions:

  • Notifications: To send local reminders about scheduled events and tasks. All notifications are generated locally on your device.
  • Microphone (optional): For voice-to-text input. All speech recognition is performed on-device when possible. If online speech recognition is required, the app will ask for your explicit consent.

Data Deletion

You can delete your data at any time through the app:

  • Delete Profile Data: Removes all data within a specific profile
  • Redo Survey: Keeps family information but resets all other data
  • Delete Profile: Permanently removes a profile and all its data
  • Uninstall App: Removes all app data from your device

Offline Operation

FamFlow is designed to work completely offline. No internet connection is required for any core functionality. All features including scheduling, task management, budget tracking, and suggestions operate entirely on your device.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the "Last Updated" date above. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
